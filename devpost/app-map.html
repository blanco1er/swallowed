Swallowed — app map

Your pet just ate something. Swallowed recognises it (photo, typing or voice), checks it for your animal in curated veterinary data, and tells you how urgent it is, why, and what to do.

The few main pieces

  • The page — public/index.html, public/styles.css, public/js/app.js: screens, navigation, the result.
  • The veterinary data — data/toxins.json (48 items × 5 animals, each with its source), data/signs.json (the "I don't know" path), data/contacts.json, data/fr.json (French texts).
  • The decision — public/js/verdict.js: pure functions that turn an item, an animal, a weight and an amount into a verdict. No AI here.
  • Recognition — server.js and src/identify.js on Render: Google Gemini says only what is in the photo or sentence, chosen from our list; it never judges danger.

One action, end to end

Tap Chocolate, enter 8 kg and 100 g → "Call a vet now".

Reference route (3 stops)

A route to follow in the code; it was summarised in conversation rather than toured step by step.

  1. The item comes in — public/js/app.js, search function pickItem and function afterItem. Every way in (tap, typing, voice, photo) ends here; afterItem asks for the pet once, asks the amount only when it changes the answer, then shows the result.
  2. The decision — public/js/verdict.js, search export function verdict and export function doseFor. The item's entry for your animal in data/toxins.json says "any amount", "no data", "low" or gives dose levels; for doses, amount × content ÷ weight is compared with those levels.
  3. The result — public/js/app.js, search function renderVerdict, function facts (the "Why this result" table), function plainSentence and function stepsFor (the three steps).

To change this, start here

  • A wording on screen → public/index.html (English) and the FR list in public/js/i18n.js (French).
  • The three steps of a verdict → const STEPS in public/js/app.js.
  • A dose level or a sign for an item → its entry in data/toxins.json (keep the source), then run npm test.
  • The emergency numbers → data/contacts.json.
  • The look → public/styles.css (colours at the top, in :root).

A practice to reuse

Turn real use into small, checkable scenarios. Typing the placeholder's own example, "ibuprofen 400", was calculated with a 200 mg tablet — half the real dose. It was found by writing down concrete situations with an expected result ("ibuprofen 400, 1 tablet, 10 kg → Call a vet now") and running them in a browser, then kept as tests (test/verdict.test.js: "ibuprofen forms carry clear labels"; test/match.test.js: "amounts in a sentence").

On another project, write a handful of "I do X, I expect to see Y" scenarios from real use before calling a feature done, and keep them as automatic checks.

Run it again

  • Online: blanco1er.github.io/swallowed (FR / EN button at the top).
  • On a computer: npm install, then npm start, then open http://localhost:3000. Photos need a Gemini key in .env (see .env.example).
  • Checks: npm test.
